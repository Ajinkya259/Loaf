What your machine is doing changes her mood — pin the CPU and she bristles; step away and she naps. Every frame comes out of a headless Blender script, so a look change is a one-command rebuild. Nothing below is hand-drawn, and at full width nothing is scaled either: the screen is a real 1280×800 and she is her real 160×128.
